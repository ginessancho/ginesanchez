Culture Decides

Culture is the residue of every past loop. It is what the organisation learned so thoroughly that it stopped noticing.

That is why it is not the soft layer around a company. It is the quiet system that decides what people notice, remember, reward, avoid, and eventually do. It usually decides first. It picks which signals count, what can be said out loud, what gets forgotten, and which consequences come back in time to change the next move.

I want this to be a practical book, in the spirit of The Lean Startup: useful to founders and teams, without reducing culture to values posters, slogans, or office rituals.

The first notes exist. If you might publish it, argue with it, or test it inside your own team, write to me.
