From the sketchbook

Bestiary

Organisms drawn over the years, rebuilt from the parts they were drawn with: a spine, and bursts, spirals, lattices, beads, combs, boxes and zigzag limbs hung on it. Each part moves the way it looks like it should. Every field after the first is bred from the one before it: two parents, their parts recombined, a little drift.
