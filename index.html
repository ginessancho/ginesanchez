Write to me ↗

A small world. What could it learn?

One creature found water. A discovery can travel.

What is happening?

A small model of discovery, communication and action. A ring marks who has heard the latest news; a blue trail means they have changed course. A grey trail follows an old location.

Each step, creatures pass their newest information to their neighbours, then act on what they heard in the previous step. Connecting the groups adds one path. Moving the water gives one creature a new discovery; everyone else must hear about it. Shapes do not determine who learns.

This world has simple rules, not answers about real societies. For inheritance and mutation, explore the full bestiary.

About +

I’m Ginés, founder of Alteridad. I trained as an anthropologist, working with Ngäbe communities in Panama on language, knowledge, and different ways of living.

Alongside the research, I made art and films to reach people an academic paper would never reach. Today I build software, write, and keep drawing. A continuing interest in what people see, what they can contribute, and what makes it possible to act together.

I want to make more room for people’s creative potential, and for a society that is beautiful to live in. Organisations are where I’m putting that ambition to work: asking whose knowledge counts, who can make a change, and how the people creating value can share in it.
