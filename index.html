How many of your decisions come back to you?

Think of the last ten decisions your team made. A decision comes back when the consequence returns to the person who decided, while they can still do something about it. Not whether it went well. Whether anyone found out.

4 of your last 10 decisions came back. The other 6 are still out there. Whatever they had to teach, nobody learned. Most decisions vanish. The organisation decides more than it learns.

Reg Revans wrote it decades ago as L ≥ C: learning must equal or exceed change, or the organism dies. Learning here is not reading. It is a consequence finding its way back. That ratio tells you what to do next: close more loops, or slow the world down. Companies do both. Governments mostly do the second.
