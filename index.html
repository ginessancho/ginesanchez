I build software and companies at Alteridad. We build technology that lowers the cost of sensing, understanding, and responding to one another—across people, organisations, machines, and living systems.

We make it cheaper to turn what is known into action—and what happens next into a better decision.

We shorten the path from understanding to action, and from consequences back to the next decision.

We make truth easier to recover, decisions easier to explain, and consequences harder to ignore.

The long game is to make it easier to create value together than to take it from somewhere else.
