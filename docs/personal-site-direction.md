# Personal site: a world that learns from its differences

Working proposal · 5 September 2026

Audience assumption: curious collaborators across research, art, and building, with useful paths for customers, funders, and publishers.

## The life’s work

**Expand what we can understand and do together, while keeping room for different ways of living.**

My reading of the work is that its continuity lies in learning across difference and giving understanding a practical form. Research uncovers ways of seeing that existing systems overlook. Art lets another audience encounter them. Software makes some of that understanding usable in everyday decisions. A book gives the method a form that others can question, practise, and teach.

Organisations are a concrete place to work on this ambition. They combine knowledge, resources, commitments, and competing interpretations. They can act on a scale that an individual cannot, but can also bury what their members know. The products address different parts of that problem.

The personal site should leave the horizon open beyond the present products. The companies provide evidence of the work; they do not have to define the limits of a life.

## The bestiary as representational art

The drawings already supply a distinctive language: assembled bodies, uneasy combinations of rigid and organic parts, imperfect lines, and populations rather than a single hero. Preserve those qualities. The next improvement is a relationship between what visitors do and what the population does.

### First experiment: how a discovery becomes shared capability

Working question: **What could this world learn?**

1. Begin with a small population. A creature encounters a new route or resource in its local surroundings. Show discovery as a brief mark or changed path, rather than a floating explanation.
2. With information confined to a small neighbourhood, the discovery benefits only those reached by it.
3. A visitor connects two groups. Knowledge can now travel across that connection. Keep bodies distinct as behaviour changes.
4. Change the environment. Old information can become wrong. A creature must discover the change, communicate it, and others must update what they do.
5. An optional explanation reveals the rules and shows what happened in this run: how many creatures received the discovery and how many changed their route successfully.

This is a proposed toy model. Its outcomes must be calculated from the stated rules, not prewritten as a moral. Connections can cost time; outdated information can spread; many identical messages need not add useful information. A connected population need not always perform better.

Keep appearance separate from intelligence, moral worth, and human demographic categories. A creature’s distinctive drawing is not a ranking. If sensing or movement abilities differ, make their situational meaning visible and explain the assignment.

The intended experience is first amusing, then surprising, then interpretable. The viewer discovers that knowing something, communicating it, and being able to act on it are different events. That gives the landing a concrete relationship to the life’s work.

### Evolution as an optional deeper experiment

The current engine already has seeded inheritance, recombination, and mutation. Parents are selected uniformly; there is no fitness-based selection. “Next generation” demonstrates lineage and drift, not adaptation to an environment.

A later experiment could ask **What are you selecting for?** A visitor changes a visible environmental demand; traits influence performance in that toy environment, and performance changes reproductive chances. Preserve ancestry and show what becomes common or disappears. A different environment can change which traits are useful.

Do not collapse biological inheritance, individual learning, and cultural transmission into one mechanism. The learning experiment changes knowledge and behaviour during a lifetime. The evolution experiment changes the distribution of inherited traits across generations.

The learning experiment is the recommended first landing interaction because it connects directly to the products and book. Breeding can remain available in the expanded bestiary.

### Diversity and demographics

The same field could later teach population composition, age structure, arrivals, departures, and chance. Those would need explicit population rules and appropriate measures. A population becoming visually varied is not evidence about real human demography.

For an educational reference, Nicky Case and Vi Hart’s *Parable of the Polygons* makes a local rule produce an observable population pattern. Borrow the sequence of interaction and discovery, with attribution if adapting its mechanisms. Develop this site’s meaning from Ginés’s work and drawings.

### The dissertation shapes

Keep the original Figure 17 in an expanded research note, with its actual setting and meaning. A simplified new diagram could show three connected spaces: inquiry, lived context, and action, with the learner moving between them. Explain that it is an adaptation.

Avoid imposing an ornamental triangle or a linear funnel on everything. The original includes exchange between academic and situated knowledge, and movement back into inquiry. The contemporary method can be written as:

**Notice → investigate → understand → consider implications → act → observe consequences → revise.**

Different perspectives can change the question and the desired outcome, not merely provide more inputs to a fixed goal.

“Moves / does / thinks” is a useful mnemonic. In fuller copy, explain “thinks” as the people, habits, interpretations, and relationships through which decisions happen.

The unifying question: **How does an organisation turn what it knows into action, and what happens next into learning?** The wider ambition adds whose knowledge counts, whose consequences are noticed, and who can influence what comes next.

An illustrative inventory example can tie the views together: a shortage becomes visible; a replenishment commitment has an owner and completion evidence; the team examines why warning signs were ignored; the result changes its next decision. Label this as a conceptual walkthrough until the relevant cross-product integration exists.

## The horsepower moment

Treat this as a proposed demonstration to earn:

**A small team can turn a question into a tested change that previously required a much larger organisation.**

Software becoming easier to produce makes the rest of the journey more consequential: choosing a valuable problem, reaching the people affected, earning trust, fitting the work, and observing the result. “Marketing and distribution are everything” is too absolute for the life’s-work story. Distribution matters because an idea that never reaches a setting where it can be understood and used has limited effect.

For one narrow operational task, measure the elapsed time and human effort from a useful signal to a completed change with an observed consequence. Compare against a documented baseline; retain quality, rework, cost, and ownership checks. A faster loop with a worse outcome is not success. Do not invent a universal organisational horsepower score or imply that the products have already demonstrated the multiplier.

The demonstration should be a concrete before/after account, such as a replenishment decision made, executed, and checked. The homepage can link to that evidence when it exists.

## How the book and distribution fit

The art can give someone an experience worth sharing. An essay can name the question behind it. The book can teach a repeatable method. The products can let a team practise parts of that method. What happens in practice supplies evidence, disagreements, and revisions for the next piece of writing.

This makes distribution part of the intellectual work: translating an idea so another audience can encounter, understand, test, and use it. It continues the public-engagement purpose of Alteridad+.

People should be able to share a particular world or experiment with its seed and settings intact. A shared link should reproduce the same logical population across screen sizes. The current implementation changes population count with viewport size, so the existing seed hash alone does not yet guarantee that property.

Keep the initial page complete at a glance. Use native expandable sections with visible labels, keyboard support, and direct links to the deeper material. Do not require playing to understand the mission or find contact details.

On entry, the bestiary can be still. Start sustained motion on “Play,” with pause and reduced-motion support. Keep the main copy readable while the field runs. A failed script must leave a usable personal page.

The smallest reviewable implementation would integrate the existing drawings into the opening, replace the long calculator exposition with the short proposed copy, and put further reading behind disclosures. Then implement and validate one learning experiment. Preserve the standalone bestiary as a place to explore generations. Larger simulation ideas remain proposals until the first interaction earns its place.
